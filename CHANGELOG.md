# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Free time (Calendar tab): every time of day can be spent on an activity or with a Confidant. Picking one writes a line in the chat box you can edit; when it is sent the effects are applied (social stat points, money, HP/SP back; bond points), the calendar moves on one time of day, the AI is told to narrate it without awarding it again, and the chat notes what it gave. Erase the line and write something else, and nothing happens. Activities come from lorebook entries titled "Activity:" (Where / When / Weather / Effect / Rain / Text), else a generic set (study, read, part-time job, work out, help out, go out, hobby, rest); rain adds bonuses. Confidant entries can say "When:" and "Where:".
- Confidant hangouts give 3 bond points, 6 with a Persona of their Arcana in the stock ("a stronger bond").
- Worn out (P5): after an Otherworld visit the next time of day is only for resting; the AI is told.
- Velvet Room: the Confidant bonus is now XP for the new Persona (30% of a level per rank, about three levels at rank 10), not flat levels; the XP left over is kept.
- Experience, Persona-style: a level costs about five fights against enemies of your level (2, 3, 3 and 4 for the first four levels), and an enemy's XP grows with its own level and category (minion ½, normal 1, elite 2½, boss 5). Story awards (small / medium / large / epic) are a share of the current level (5 / 15 / 35 / 75%, editable), so they weigh the same at every level. The pace is a percentage in Settings. Records from the old curve keep their level and their progress into it.
- Personas level on their own for a Wild Card: the one out earns the fight's XP (also from Shuffle Time Wands), the others only with Growth 1/2/3 (25/50/100%); level-ups learn skills and show in the fight summary. Anyone who is not a Wild Card is as strong as their Persona: its level and theirs move together.
- A Persona can pass its user's level (fusion with Confidant bonus levels, its own XP). The level rule is on obtaining it: fusing, drawing it in Shuffle Time or adding it at its base level needs the user to have reached that base level (an awakening, or one added before that, comes in at the user's level).
- With a Persona, characters get no attribute points on level-up: their stats grow with the Persona, as in the games.
- Fights: a Wild Card changes Persona on their turn (free, once a turn, from the new Persona button): its attributes, affinities and skills replace the old one's, HP and SP stay; the Persona out at the end stays equipped.
- Fix: adding a Persona by hand, the Compendium suggestions can be searched by name in Firefox too (it matched only the "Arcana · Lv" label).
- Fix: enemies were built on the old Better Stats scale (about 10 in every attribute at level 1) while characters follow their Personas (about 3 at level 1), so even a level 1 minion was out of reach. Enemies now grow like the Compendium's Personas (~3 at level 1, ~14 at 20, ~32 at 50, ~57 at 90); normal enemies have a little less HP and elites trade attributes for HP, keeping the same odds at every level (one normal: easy; two normals or an elite: a real fight alone; a boss: a party's job).
- Persona look (on by default, Settings → Theme): Persona 4 style for the Stats panel, fights, Shuffle Time, dungeons and the chat — black, one accent, heavy slanted italics, TV-static title bars. Presets Yellow (P4) / Blue (P3) / Red (P5) plus your own accent, HP and SP colours; the pop-out follows. The Velvet Room stays blue.
- Chat with the Persona look: the scene header becomes a date strip (4/14 Th · time of day · weather · place) on the last reply; a card when the day changes and a banner when the place changes; under a reply, the social stats it raised and the bonds it formed or ranked up (kept on the message, per swipe).
- Social stats with the Persona look: the list with rank pips and titles replaces the P5 circles.
- Fix: Shuffle Time's cards shared a class with the fighters' cards, turning the fighters cream-coloured and narrow in the fight window.
- The Stats panel shows each Persona skill's cost from the games instead of the old Light/Medium/Heavy tiers.
- A Persona added by hand is never refused for its level: one above the character comes in at the character's level. The add logs its outcome (added / refused and why), and says so when the chat has nothing to save into.
- A Persona added by hand without a level comes in at its user's level (like an awakening) instead of being refused when the Compendium's level is higher; a level typed above the user's is still refused.
- Fix: lorebooks bound to the character card or the chat (not only the globally active ones) are now read for the Persona Compendium, Confidants, Calendar and Otherworld entries; adding a Persona by name re-reads the Compendium first when the name is not found.
- Otherworld: places kept in the chat. Labyrinths (blocks that open at the start / on a date / after a domain / by hand / from the story, fresh floors each visit, depth-based levels, block guardians and checkpoints, requests, the Reaper) and domains (fixed map of 2-4 areas kept between visits, safe-room checkpoints, ruler, deadline on the calendar: missed it closes with a story notice, cleared it stays open to revisit). Every visit takes a time of day. From `Dungeon:` lorebook entries, the story's `otherworld` key (swipe undo) or by hand.
- Shuffle Time (P3/P4) after won fights: chance with bonuses, four cards shown then shuffled face down, Persona cards (from the fight or near your level, base level, never above yours) and the Minor Arcana (Cups heal, Coins money, Wands XP, Swords a weapon); the fight's story mentions the card.
- Awakenings: a Persona the AI awakens can be any Persona (any Arcana, any game, or an original one) and starts at exactly its user's level, up or down; the prompt no longer lists the 60 weakest Compendium names (only a small homebrew Compendium is listed).
- Calendar: "Read the date from the chat" for chats already going (last Info Box date or story date header); the empty-state text says where the date comes from.
- Calendar: a date without a year but with a weekday ("APRIL 1 — TUESDAY") lands on the nearest year where that weekday is right, instead of none; with no Info Box date, a date header the story writes itself (month, day and weekday together, plus a lone time-of-day line) starts or moves the calendar.
- Removed the Doom Counter (tension score in every reply, plot-twist picker, trap mode) and the characters' Knives, with their settings, prompts and Workshop tab; old settings are cleaned up on load.
- The Info Box is optional: without it the calendar carries date, time of day, weather and place (AI key, prompt, context, weather effects, scene header, editable place). The separate tracker call no longer skips when the Info Box, thoughts and quests are all off but RPG mode is on.
- Calendar: real dates with four times of day, in charge of the Info Box date and time (forward only from the AI, any way by hand), weather by day with a forecast, events and deadlines with countdowns (story, by hand, `Calendar:` lorebook entries), P5-style tab in the Stats panel and a date chip; swipe undo.
- Confidant abilities for teammates: Baton Pass (now needs the bond when Confidants are on), Follow Up, Endure, Harisen Recovery, Protect and the Persona Awakening at rank 10; ranks can be set by lorebook unlock lines; shown in the Confidants tab and told to the AI.
- Velvet Room: fuse a Wild Card's Personas — P5R Arcana chart (P4G / P3R for Aeon and Jester), same-Arcana, special (2–6 Personas) and treasure fusions, level limit, maxed-Confidant unlocks, Confidant bonus levels, skill inheritance by type with your own pick; list of every fusion the stock can make. Compendium entries now carry `Inherits:`.
- Checks roll on the social stats (Knowledge, Guts, Proficiency, Kindness, Charm): d20 + 2 per rank above 1 (up to +8) + situational bonus (±5); a natural 1 always fails and the chance is capped at 95%, so nothing is ever certain. People other than the user count as rank 2; old requests naming battle stats are mapped to the closest social stat. Dungeon traps and locks use them too.
- Confidants: lorebook format, ranks with requirements and unlocks, per-card mode (automatic / defined / mixed / sandbox), affinity gate, one per Arcana, optional Accept / Reject, AI points with swipe undo, tarot-card tab in the Stats panel.
- A Persona can never be above its user's level (Levels on): adding or equipping one refused, the AI's stronger awakenings come in at the user's level, + capped.
- Hit chances raised (about 90% between equals, as in the games).
- HP and SP shown and edited as points (current / max) in the Stats panel, fights and dungeons; max HP from En and level, max SP from level; SP costs are the game's points.
- Removed the automatic conditions from low bars (Badly wounded, Hungry…); old ones are cleared from chats.
- Persona skill database (P5R + P3R + P4G, 1199 skills) used by fights.
- Persona combat: elements and affinities, knock-downs, 1 More, Baton Pass, All-Out Attack, HP/SP costs, ailments and technicals, buffs, walls, mirrors; Shadows from the Compendium; fixed the inspect panel's attribute labels.
- Compendium entries say how each Persona is obtained: special fusion ingredients, requirements and unlock conditions.
- Persona Compendium lorebook: P5R (242) plus the P3 Reload / Aigis / FES and P4 Golden Personas P5R lacks (86), 328 in all, and the script that builds it.
- Personas: St/Ma/En/Ag/Lu from the equipped Persona, affinities (with Gun) and skills, Wild Card, level-ups, Compendium read from lorebook entries, AI awakening with program-set numbers, Persona tab in the Stats panel.
- Stats panel: HP, SP and attributes in one tab as Persona 5-style bars.
- Social stats (Knowledge, Guts, Proficiency, Kindness, Charm) with ranks and titles, drawn as circles.
- Sphere grid removed.
- Combat and effect numbers rebalanced for the 1–99 scale.
- Initial fork of Better Stats as Persona Stats (see README for the list of changes).
- Earlier history belongs to Better Stats / Doom's Enhancement Suite.
